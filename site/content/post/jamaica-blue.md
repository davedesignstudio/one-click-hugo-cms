We expect the boards to reach working moisture by early spring. If you’ve been waiting on a dining table or a run of built-ins in pale oak, now is a good time to get on the list.

White oak has long been a favorite for furniture that needs strength and quiet grain. Straight, even boards from this load will suit tabletops, shelf systems, and cabinet faces.

## A little context

Air-drying with stickers keeps the wood moving slowly and evenly. We still kiln-check critical stock, but this batch is settling the old-fashioned way under cover in the shed.

> The best furniture starts before the first cut — with lumber that has been given time.

We’ll keep a few of the widest boards for a single dining table commission. The rest will go toward cabinetry and benches.

## What you need to know before reserving

Lead times for spring work are filling. A deposit holds your place in the queue and locks in species and rough dimensions. Finishes and final joinery details get locked during the design visit.

Email the shop or use the contact form if you’d like to see sample boards in person.
