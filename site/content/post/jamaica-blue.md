We're excited to share photos from our latest custom kitchen cabinet project — a full remodel featuring white oak cabinetry with soft-close hardware and a hand-rubbed oil finish.

## The project

Our client wanted a warm, inviting kitchen that felt both modern and timeless. After several design meetings, we settled on flat-panel doors in quarter-sawn white oak, open shelving on one wall, and a large island with a butcher-block top.

## The build

Every cabinet was built in our shop using traditional frame-and-panel construction with dovetail drawer boxes and Blum soft-close hinges and slides. The oak was finished with a custom blend of tung oil and wax that brings out the wood's natural figure while providing excellent moisture resistance.

Key features include:

- Floor-to-ceiling pantry with pull-out organizers
- Under-cabinet LED lighting with concealed wiring
- Custom range hood surround in matching oak
- Soft-close drawers and doors throughout

## The result

The finished kitchen transformed the space — warm wood tones against white quartz countertops and subway tile backsplash create a look that's both contemporary and enduring.
