This week a long walnut slab cleared the clamps after glue-up and leveling. The
client asked for a quiet dining table—no live edge drama, just a calm field of
grain and legs that tuck neatly under.

## From slab to surface

We flattened both faces, checked moisture, and cut breadboard ends with
elongated pegs so seasonal movement has a place to go. The underside got the
same attention as the top; a table gets flipped more often than people admit
during moves.

## Finish plan

Two coats of hardwax oil, hand-rubbed, with a third maintenance coat after
delivery. The color will deepen for the first few months in the dining room’s
south light.
