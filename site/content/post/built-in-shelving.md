Every home has them — the awkward alcove under the stairs, the narrow wall beside the fireplace, the sloped ceiling in the attic bedroom. These spaces are often ignored or filled with a generic bookshelf that never quite fits. Custom built-in shelving is one of the most satisfying ways to reclaim that space.

## Why built-ins beat freestanding furniture

A built-in is measured to the millimeter. It follows the contours of your walls, integrates with your baseboards and crown molding, and looks like it was always part of the house. Freestanding furniture, no matter how nice, always leaves gaps and feels like an afterthought.

## Common projects we build

- **Living room media walls** with concealed cable routing and adjustable shelving
- **Home office nooks** with desk surfaces, file drawers, and upper cabinets
- **Bedroom window seats** with storage underneath and flanking bookcases
- **Hallway mudroom units** with hooks, cubbies, and a bench for shoes

## The process

We start with a site visit to measure and photograph the space. Then we produce a sketch or 3D rendering for your approval before any wood is cut. Most built-in projects take two to four weeks from approval to installation, depending on complexity.

## A recent example

Last month we built floor-to-ceiling bookcases in a sloped attic bedroom. The left side follows the ceiling angle perfectly; the right side is full height. LED strip lighting on each shelf makes the space feel like a proper library instead of a storage afterthought.
