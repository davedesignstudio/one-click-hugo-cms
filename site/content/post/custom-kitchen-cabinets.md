We just wrapped up one of our favorite projects of the year: a complete set of custom kitchen cabinets in quarter-sawn white oak for a 1920s bungalow in South San Francisco.

## The challenge

The existing kitchen had mismatched stock cabinets from three different remodels over the decades. The layout wasted corner space, the drawers stuck, and nothing was quite level. The homeowners wanted a cohesive look that honored the home's character without feeling dated.

## Our approach

We started with detailed measurements and a full layout sketch. Every cabinet was built in our shop, then dry-fitted on site before final installation. We used soft-close hinges, dovetail drawer boxes, and a hand-rubbed oil finish that brings out the oak's natural grain.

## The result

The new kitchen feels twice as large. Deep pull-out drawers replaced awkward corner cabinets, a custom pantry unit hides the water heater, and the continuous wood grain across all door fronts ties the whole room together.
