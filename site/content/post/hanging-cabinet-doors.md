Cabinet doors look simple until a humid August and a dry January argue about the gap. Here’s how we keep reveals even.

## Hinge choice matters

Soft-close European hinges give us six-way adjustment. We set them after the boxes are installed and scribed, not before—walls are never as square as drawings.

## The reveal ritual

We aim for a consistent 2–3mm reveal. Doors get final planing after hanging so the eye reads one clean line across a run of cabinets.

>Never rush the last pass with a block plane. That last thousandth is what you notice every morning.

If a door warps slightly after finishing, we correct with hinge depth and a light re-fit rather than fighting the wood.
