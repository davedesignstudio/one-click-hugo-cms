A beautiful piece of furniture or cabinetry isn't complete until it's properly finished. The finishing process protects the wood from moisture, wear, and UV damage while enhancing its natural color and grain.

## The finishing process

**Sanding** is the foundation of any good finish. We progress through progressively finer grits — typically starting at 120 and working up to 220 or higher — to create a smooth, even surface that accepts finish uniformly.

**Staining** adds color while still allowing the grain to show through. We test stains on scrap pieces cut from the same lumber to ensure the color matches your expectations before applying it to the finished piece.

**Sealing** with a topcoat protects the wood and gives it the desired sheen. Options include:

- **Oil finishes** (tung oil, linseed oil) penetrate the wood and provide a natural, low-sheen look
- **Varnish and polyurethane** create a durable film finish ideal for tabletops and high-wear surfaces
- **Lacquer** dries quickly and can be buffed to a high gloss or rubbed to a satin sheen
- **Wax** adds a soft luster and can be applied over other finishes for added protection

## Why finishing matters

The right finish can transform a good piece into a great one. It highlights the wood's character, protects your investment, and ensures your furniture or cabinetry looks beautiful for years to come.
