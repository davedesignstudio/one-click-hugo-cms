A custom kitchen is a significant investment, and knowing the process helps everything go smoothly. Here's how we typically work through a cabinetry project from start to finish.

## Consultation and design

We start with a conversation about how you use your kitchen—storage needs, layout preferences, and style direction. We measure the space carefully and discuss materials, hardware, and timeline.

## Shop drawings and approval

Before we cut any wood, you'll review drawings showing cabinet dimensions, door styles, and placement. This step ensures everyone is aligned before fabrication begins.

## Fabrication in the shop

Cabinets are built in our workshop where we control joinery, sanding, and finishing. Building off-site means less disruption in your home and higher-quality results in a controlled environment.

## Installation and finishing touches

On install day, we fit each cabinet, level and secure boxes, hang doors, and install hardware. We walk through the finished work with you and address any final adjustments.
