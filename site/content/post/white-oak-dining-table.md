A client asked for a table that could host twelve without feeling heavy. White oak answered: strong, stable when quartersawn, and warm under oil.

## Why quartersawn

Quartersawn stock moves less across the width of a board and shows a quieter, straighter figure. For a long top, that stability matters more than flashy grain.

>The base is a pair of trestles joined with through-tenons—visible, honest, and easy to tighten years from now.

We’ll glue the top from matched boards, leave a breadboard end to keep the ends true, and finish with a hardwax oil that can be refreshed without stripping.

## What to expect

If you’re commissioning a table, bring how you actually use the room: kids, homework, holidays. Those details shape thickness, edge profile, and how the base clears knees.
