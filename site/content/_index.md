# Anthem for Carpentry

---

## I

From forest floor to finished door,  
We honor every grain —  
Each knot a story, each board a shore  
Where patience meets the plane.

---

## II

The chisel finds the hidden line,  
The saw sings straight and true,  
We level, plumb, and true align  
What measuring can do.

---

## Chorus

*We measure twice and cut once clear,*  
*We join with heart and hand —*  
*What we build outlasts the builders,*  
*And stands where others stand.*

---

## III

No nail where a dovetail belongs,  
No shortcut in the glue —  
We let the timber guide our songs  
And work the whole day through.

---

## IV

From rafter high to threshold low,  
From hinge to final coat,  
We send our finest work below  
For others to devote.

---

## Final Chorus

*We measure twice and cut once clear,*  
*We join with heart and hand —*  
*What we build outlasts the builders,*  
*And stands where others stand.*

---

## Amen

**Measure. Cut. Join. Finish.**

*The anthem of the carpenter.*
